Support

Questions, problems, or ideas? Email claythecreator9@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you as soon as we can. It helps to tell us what you were doing, what happened, and which iPhone and iOS version you have. Please don’t send medical information.

Good to know

  • Your data lives only on your phone. In the app, open Settings and tap “Back up my data” to save a copy to Files or iCloud Drive.
  • Moving to a new phone? Back up on the old phone. On the new one, choose “I already have a backup” when you first open Macrivo, or use Settings, then “Restore from a backup.”
  • Erasing everything: Settings, then “Erase all data.” This can’t be undone, so back up first if you want a copy.
  • Reminders not arriving? Check iPhone Settings, then Notifications, then Macrivo, and make sure no Focus mode is silencing them.
  • Check the numbers. Food data comes from the U.S. Department of Agriculture and Open Food Facts and can contain errors. Check the package when it matters, and edit any food’s numbers in the app.

Macrivo is a tracking tool, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet.
