Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Macrivo (“the app”) is a food, macro, and water tracker made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: what you log stays on your phone.

The short version

  • Macrivo has no accounts and no servers of its own. What you log stays on your phone.
  • We don’t sell or share your information, and the app has no ads, analytics tools, or trackers.
  • When you search for foods online, the words you type are sent to the U.S. Department of Agriculture’s FoodData Central to find matches.
  • When you scan or type a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to find the product.
  • You can back up, export, or erase your data at any time in Settings.

What the app stores on your phone

The foods and meals you log, their amounts and dates, your saved meals and recipes, water, goals and day types, nutrient values, reminders, and settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. We can’t see this information.

Some of it, such as what you eat and the goals you set, can be considered health-related. It stays on your device and we don’t receive it. If you delete the app, this information is deleted, unless you saved a backup file yourself.

What leaves your phone

Online food searches

When you search for a food online, the text you type, and requests for details about a food you pick, are sent to the USDA FoodData Central service through the U.S. government’s api.data.gov service, using an access key that belongs to the app. As with any internet request, the request includes your device’s IP address, and those services may keep standard server logs under their own policies. We don’t receive these requests and don’t store them on any server of ours. Your logs, goals, and personal details are never included.

Barcode lookups

When you scan or type a product barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a non-profit open food database, to find the product. As with any internet request, it includes your device’s IP address, and Open Food Facts may keep standard server logs under its own policies. We don’t receive these requests. Only the number is sent: camera images never leave your phone, and your logs, goals, and personal details are never included.

Backups and exports

The app can create a backup file or a spreadsheet export, but only when you ask it to. The file is created on your device, and you choose where it goes using the iPhone share sheet (for example Files, iCloud Drive, or email). We never receive these files. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone and delivered by your phone. No server of ours is involved.

Apple

If you downloaded Macrivo from the App Store or TestFlight, Apple handles the download and processes your information under its own terms and privacy policy. If you’ve chosen to share analytics and crash data with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage statistics. We can’t tell who they come from.

Camera

The camera is optional and used only by the barcode scanner, to read a barcode in the live camera view. Pictures and video are not saved, recorded, or sent anywhere. You can turn camera access off at any time in iPhone Settings, and you can type a barcode number instead.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and used only to show the reminders you set up. They are scheduled and delivered on your phone. You can turn them off in iPhone Settings.

What we don’t do

We don’t create accounts, track you across other apps or websites, show ads, run analytics, or sell or share your information.

Third-party services

The app uses these services, each with its own privacy practices that we don’t control:

  • U.S. Department of Agriculture FoodData Central (fdc.nal.usda.gov), reached through api.data.gov, for online food searches.
  • Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) for barcode lookups.
  • Apple, for downloading and updating the app.

Children

Macrivo isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. The app doesn’t collect personal information at all. Because the app tracks food, we encourage anyone under 18 to use it with the guidance of a parent or guardian.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings, then Back up & export.
  • Erase: Settings, then Erase all data, or delete the app.
  • Permissions: turn camera and notification access on or off in iPhone Settings.

Because we don’t hold your data, we can’t retrieve, restore, or delete it for you. Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights over your personal information. Because we don’t collect it, there is generally nothing for us to provide, but you can contact us with any question.

Security

Your data stays in your device’s protected app storage. Protect your phone with a passcode. Backup files you create are not encrypted.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that handle information differently, such as accounts, purchases, advertising, syncing, or new permissions, we’ll update this policy before they are released and change the date above.
